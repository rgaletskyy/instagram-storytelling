# HealthyDoggo — Story Telling Rules

Companion to `healthydoggo-story-design-rules.md`. That file governs **how it looks**. This one governs **what it says and in what order**.

---

## 1. Core principles

1. **One story = one idea.** If a slide needs two headlines, it's two slides.
2. **Sell the outcome, not the product.** Not "salmon oil with Omega-3" — "shiny coat, no itching".
3. **Talk to one owner, not an audience.** Second person singular ("твій хвостик"), never "шановні клієнти".
4. **The dog is the hero, the brand is the guide.** We solve the owner's guilt/worry; we are not the protagonist.
5. **Earn every next tap.** Each slide must open a small loop the next slide closes.
6. **Show, then claim.** UGC, before/after and real hands beat adjectives.

---

## 2. Sequence structure (3–7 slides)

| # | Slide | Job | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | Stop the thumb. Problem, mistake, or question. | ≤ 1 s to read |
| 2 | **Tension** | Why it matters / what happens if ignored. | 2–3 s |
| 3 | **Solution** | The product as the answer. First mention of the brand. | 3 s |
| 4 | **Proof** | UGC, review, comparison, vet logic, before/after. | 3 s |
| 5 | **Offer** | Price, set, discount, deadline. | 2 s |
| 6 | **CTA** | One action. Link sticker. | 1 s |

**Minimum viable sequence:** Hook → Solution → CTA (3 slides).
**Never exceed 7.** Completion rate collapses after that.

---

## 3. Hook rules (slide 1 decides everything)

Use one of these five:
- **Mistake** — "3 помилки, через які паличка стає небезпечною"
- **Question to self** — "Чи помічаєш ознаки старіння у твого хвостика?"
- **Number promise** — "ТОП-3 проблеми у собак восени"
- **Curiosity gap** — "Знаєш, що на фото?"
- **Objection head-on** — "А цуцику можна?"

Hook must be **≤ 7 words**, contain a concrete noun, and never mention the price or the brand.

---

## 4. Engagement rhythm

- **1 interactive sticker per sequence**, placed on slide 2 or 3 — not the first (too early) or last (competes with the CTA).
- Poll/quiz answers ≤ 3 words, both options must feel socially safe to pick.
- **Reply to DMs from a question sticker publicly** in the next day's stories — this is the highest-converting format we have (see §7.3 archetype).
- Repost customer stories within 24 h. Always with a thank-you, never with a sales line attached.

---

## 5. CTA rules

- **One CTA per sequence.** Never two links.
- Verb-first, lowercase, 1–2 words: `замовити`, `дивитись`, `забрати знижку`.
- Give a reason to act now: stock, deadline, season, price.
- Intermediate slides use micro-cues only (`дивіться далі`, arrow) — not links.
- After the CTA slide, **stop**. Don't append an extra slide.

---

## 6. Content mix (weekly)

| Share | Type |
|---|---|
| 40% | Educational / care advice (no selling) |
| 25% | Product & benefit |
| 20% | Social proof, UGC, reviews, DM answers |
| 10% | Behind the scenes, team, shelter/charity |
| 5% | Promo & discounts |

**Rule:** never post two selling sequences back to back. Give value first, ask second.

---

## 7. Tone of voice

- Warm, friendly, plainspoken. A knowledgeable friend, not a clinic.
- **Ukrainian only.** Product names stay in original spelling.
- Short sentences. No jargon unless immediately explained.
- **No fear-mongering, no medical promises, no guarantees of cure.** Say "допомагає", not "лікує".
- Emotion comes from the dog, not from adjectives about the product.

---

## 8. QA before publishing a sequence

1. Can slide 1 be understood in 1 second with sound off?
2. Does every slide open or close a loop?
3. Is there exactly one CTA, on the last slide?
4. Is there exactly one interactive sticker?
5. Is the value-to-sell ratio at least 2:1 across the sequence?
6. Would an owner who never buys still find slides 1–3 useful?
7. Is the sequence ≤ 7 slides?
